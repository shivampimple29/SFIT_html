PRIVACY POLICY

At Nitya Foundation, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, share, and safeguard your data. We may collect personal details such as your name, email, phone number, postal address, and donation history when you engage with us through donations, volunteering, or our website. Additionally, we collect non-personal data, including IP addresses and website usage statistics, to improve user experience. We use your information to process donations, provide tax receipts, communicate updates, and enhance our services. Your data is never sold or rented, though we may share it with trusted third-party service providers for payment processing, IT support, and legal compliance. We implement strict security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access or misuse. You have the right to access, update, or request the deletion of your personal information, subject to legal obligations. You may also opt out of marketing communications. Our website uses cookies to enhance user experience, and you can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings. While we may provide links to third-party websites, we are not responsible for their privacy policies. We may update this Privacy Policy periodically, and any changes will be posted on our website with the revised date.
