Intoduction:

Avengers: Endgame is a Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU) film that concludes the Infinity Saga. It follows the remaining Avengers as they attempt to reverse the destruction caused by Thanos .

Plot:

  • Thanos uses the Infinity Stones to erase half of all life in the universe.
  • Carol Danvers rescues Tony Stark and Nebula from deep space.
  • The remaining Avengers reunite on Earth.
  • They find Thanos on an uninhabited planet and plan to use the Infinity Stones to reverse his actions.
  • They discover that Thanos has destroyed the Infinity Stones.
  • Thor decapitates Thanos.
  • The Avengers attempt to travel back in time to retrieve the Stones.
  • Tony Stark reluctantly agrees to help.

Director:

-Joe Russo
-Anthony Russo

Release:

  • Avengers: Endgame premiered at the Los Angeles Convention Center on April 22, 2019.
  • It was released in theaters in the India on April 26,2019.
